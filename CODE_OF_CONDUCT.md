# Code of Conduct

The universe is 93 billion light years wide. There is room for everyone, so let's keep it a nice place to be.

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome in JUniverse, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Gravity treats every mass the same way. So do we.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient. Everybody was a beginner once, even Kepler.
* Respect different opinions and experiences. Lemaître and Hoyle disagreed about the beginning of everything and stayed polite about it.
* Give feedback on the code, never on the person who wrote it.
* Accept feedback gracefully. A failing test is not a personal attack, it's reality talking.
* Own your mistakes and learn from them. NASA did, after the Mars Climate Orbiter.
* Focus on what is best for the project and the people around it.

## What we don't accept

* Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
* Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
* Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
* Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
* Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Black holes absorb everything. We don't have to.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any place where someone represents JUniverse.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer at **[contact address]**. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious the behavior is, the maintainer may:

1. **Remind**: a private message explaining what went wrong.
2. **Warn**: a formal warning, with a clear explanation of what happens next time.
3. **Suspend**: a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Ban**: a permanent ban. Some orbits are not meant to come back.
